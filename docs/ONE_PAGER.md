# GrowthOS — One Page

**Agentic-HCM enablement for growth, coaching, skills, and workforce readiness.**

---

## One-liner

GrowthOS helps organizations move from traditional HR service delivery to **AI-first dynamic enablement**—with governed agents, explainable recommendations, and humans owning employment decisions.

**Tagline:** Grow the individual. Elevate the manager. Transform the organization.

---

## Category

**Agentic HCM enablement layer** — beside your HRIS system of record.

Not payroll, benefits, or case management today. Not a generic HR chatbot.

---

## Problem

- Employees lack clear growth paths tied to real skills and roles.
- Managers lack talent intelligence; they work around HR systems.
- HR is stuck in ticket mode with untrusted skills data.
- Legacy stacks capture transactions, not how work and skills actually change.

---

## Solution

One governed layer connecting:

| Stakeholder | What they get |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Employees** | Growth profile, career paths, skill gaps, 30/60/90 plan, 1:1 prep |
| **Managers** | Team skills, coaching agent, stretch ideas, capability planning |
| **HR** | Skills readiness, mobility insights, talent density, workforce readiness |

**Six agents** + **Governance Agent** on every response. Every recommendation: explanation, confidence, evidence.

---

## Trust layer

Blocks termination, layoff, compensation, promotion, hiring, performance rating, succession, and punitive labels. Demo: **Demo: governance block** on growth profile.
